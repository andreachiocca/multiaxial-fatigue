% registry: COIN
\methodhead{COIN}{Schmidt and Kraft (2023): complex invariants, the COIN LiWI method}\label{sheet:COIN}

\block{Source} Schmidt and Kraft, \emph{Int.\ J.\ Fatigue} 168 (2023) 107474 \cite{schmidt2023},
Eqs.~(21)--(32), pp.~5--6; Tables~3--4.

\block{Idea and scope} The quadratic invariant form of the Modified Mohr--Mises hypothesis,
$I_1^2-k_a^2I_2$, is written in complex stress-amplitude space for single-frequency (synchronous
sinusoidal) multiaxial loading. Two complex invariants, a Hermitian magnitude $H$ and a complex square
$C$, are blended by a phase-sensitivity weight $k_\phi$; the weight decides how much a phase shift
between the invariant contributions lowers the equivalent amplitude. The source handles mean stresses
separately, in its damage laws.

\block{Formulation} With $k_a=s_{-1}/\tm$ (Eq.~22), the quadratic Mertens form (Eq.~21) is
\begin{equation}
\sigma_{\mathrm{eq},ka}^2 = I_1^2 - k_a^2\,I_2 .
\label{eq:coin-q}
\end{equation}
For $\Tsig(t)=\operatorname{Re}(\mathbf Z e^{j\omega t})$ it defines
\begin{equation}
H = |\tr\mathbf Z|^2 - \frac{k_a^2}{2}\Bigl(|\tr\mathbf Z|^2 - \tr(\mathbf Z\,\overline{\mathbf Z})\Bigr) ,
\qquad
C = (\tr\mathbf Z)^2 - \frac{k_a^2}{2}\Bigl((\tr\mathbf Z)^2 - \tr(\mathbf Z^2)\Bigr) ,
\label{eq:coin-hc}
\end{equation}
combined into the equivalent amplitude (Eq.~32)
\begin{equation}
\sigma_{\mathrm{COIN}} = \sqrt{k_\phi\,H + (1-k_\phi)\,|C|} .
\label{eq:coin-main}
\end{equation}
They satisfy $H=2\,\overline Q$ and $|C|=2\,(\max_t Q-\overline Q)$: $k_\phi=1$ gives the invariant RMS,
$k_\phi=0.5$ the time-domain maximum $\sqrt{\max_t Q}$ (the amplitude of MMMH,
Sheet~\ref{sheet:MMMH}), and $k_\phi=0$ cancels invariants shifted by $90^\circ$. The paper derives
Eqs.~(24)--(30) for plane stress.

\block{Constants and calibration} Paper: $k_a$ from the two fatigue limits; $k_\phi$ fitted empirically
per material (Table~3 reports 0.85--1.10), with no identification rule. Repository: $k_a$ from the
workbook $\fm$, $\tm$ (finite and positive); $k_\phi$ read from the optional constant
\texttt{k\_phi}, default 1.0. Only the master-curve constants are fitted.

\block{Life} No native life law in the repository. The index $\sigma_{\mathrm{COIN}}/s_{-1}$ is the
damage parameter of the shared master curve (Section~\ref{sec:mastercurve}).

\block{Repository choices}
\begin{itemize}
\item $\mathbf Z=\Sc-j\,\Ss$. The plane-stress Eqs.~(21)--(32) carry over to 3-D stress tensors through
  $\tr\mathbf Z$, $\tr(\mathbf Z\overline{\mathbf Z})$ and $\tr(\mathbf Z^2)$ without change.
\item Default $k_\phi=1.0$ (RMS formulation) when \texttt{k\_phi} is absent, since the paper gives no rule.
\item No mean-stress term, as in the paper's equivalent amplitude (the source injects the mean stress
  through its PRAM/PRAJ damage laws, which are not implemented).
\item The returned value is the index $\sigma_{\mathrm{COIN}}/s_{-1}$; a negative radicand is clamped
  to~0.
\end{itemize}

\block{Worked example} Steel with $s_{-1}=313.9$, $\tm=196.2\MPa$; bending $\sigma_a=200\MPa$ and torsion
$\tau_a=120\MPa$ at $90^\circ$: index $0.8832$ with $k_\phi=1.0$ and $0.6371$ with $k_\phi=0.5$.

\block{Verification} The paper prints only scatter-band statistics of lives on external data (Table~4),
no specimen table, so no table is reproduced. Checked: the plane-stress closed forms Eqs.~(27) and~(30)
for arbitrary component phases; $k_\phi=1$ against $\sqrt{2\,\overline Q}$ and $k_\phi=0.5$ against
$\sqrt{\max_t Q}$ from a 20\,000-step time-domain evaluation; exact cancellation at $k_\phi=0$ for
$\sigma_x=k_a\tau_{xy}$ shifted by $90^\circ$; index~1 at both fatigue limits.
