% registry: PBP
\methodhead{PBP}{Cristofori, Susmel and Tovo (2008): projection-by-projection criterion}\label{sheet:PBP}

\block{Source} A.~Cristofori, L.~Susmel, R.~Tovo, \emph{Int.\ J.\ Fatigue} 30 (2008) 1646--1658
\cite{cristofori2008}, Eqs.~(4)--(13), (A.3), Appendix~B.

\block{Idea and scope} The deviatoric stress is written as a 5-vector whose norm squared is $J_2$. Its
path is projected on the principal directions of its covariance matrix; each projection is cycle
counted, and the projection amplitudes are recombined quadratically into an equivalent deviatoric
amplitude $\Gamma$. $\Gamma$ is compared with a reference strength that is linear in the ratio of the
hydrostatic stress to $\Gamma$. Proposed for fatigue damage under multiaxial constant and variable
amplitude loading, proportional and non-proportional; the repository uses the constant-amplitude,
single-harmonic, fatigue-limit form.

\block{Formulation} Combining the projection amplitudes gives (Eqs.~12--13)
\begin{equation}
\Gamma = \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{5}\Bigl(\sqrt{J_{2a,i}}\Bigr)^2} ,
\label{eq:pbp-gamma}
\end{equation}
with the hydrostatic stress ratio (Eq.~6) and the reference fatigue strength (Eq.~8)
\begin{equation}
\rho_{\mathrm{ref}} = \frac{\sqrt3\,\sigma_{H,\mathrm{ref}}}{\Gamma} , \qquad
\sqrt{J_{2A,\rho}} = \tm + \rho_{\mathrm{ref}}\Bigl(\frac{\fm}{\sqrt3} - \tm\Bigr) .
\label{eq:pbp-ref}
\end{equation}
Fully reversed axial loading gives $\rho_{\mathrm{ref}}=1$ and $\sqrt{J_{2A,1}}=\fm/\sqrt3$; fully
reversed torsion gives $\rho_{\mathrm{ref}}=0$ and $\sqrt{J_{2A,0}}=\tm$. The repository returns the
index $\mathrm{DP}=\Gamma/\sqrt{J_{2A,\rho}}$, equal to 1 at both limits.

\block{Constants and calibration} Paper and repository: no fitted constant at the fatigue limit;
Eq.~\eqref{eq:pbp-ref} uses $\fm$ and $\tm$ only. There are no optional constants. Only the
master-curve constants are fitted.

\block{Life} No native life law in the repository: the paper's finite-life Eqs.~(9)--(11) with $k_\rho$
are not implemented. The index is the damage parameter of the shared master curve
(Section~\ref{sec:mastercurve}).

\block{Repository choices}
\begin{itemize}
\item Constant-amplitude single harmonic: for $\mathbf s(t)=\mathbf a\sin\omega t+\mathbf b\cos\omega t$,
  every orthonormal projection has one harmonic cycle of amplitude
  $\sqrt{(\mathbf e_i\cdot\mathbf a)^2+(\mathbf e_i\cdot\mathbf b)^2}$, so
  $\Gamma^2=J_2(\mathbf A)+J_2(\mathbf B)$ in any basis, without the covariance eigendecomposition. This
  $\Gamma$ is the $\sqrt{J_{2,a}}$ of Eq.~\eqref{eq:invariants}.
\item $\sigma_{H,\mathrm{ref}}=\sigma_{H,a}+\sigma_{H,m}$.
\item The $\sqrt3$ of Eq.~(6) is kept (Eq.~B.5 omits it), so reversed axial loading gives
  $\rho_{\mathrm{ref}}=1$.
\item The index is 0 when $\Gamma=0$ and $\infty$ when $\sqrt{J_{2A,\rho}}\le0$.
\end{itemize}

\block{Worked example} Steel with $\fm=313.9$, $\tm=196.2\MPa$; bending $\sigma_a=200\MPa$ and torsion
$\tau_a=120\MPa$: index $0.8962$ both in phase and at $90^\circ$ (no phase effect for one harmonic).

\block{Verification} The paper prints only W\"ohler constants (Table~1) and figures, so no table is
reproduced. Checked: the 5-vector norm identity $\lVert\mathbf s\rVert^2=J_2$; the covariance-projection
procedure on an 8000-point sampled path against the closed form $\Gamma$; the calibration of
Eqs.~(6)--(8) (index~1 at both limits); phase-shift invariance in bending--torsion (the closed form
Eq.~B.9 has no phase either); the mean-stress dependence through $\rho_{\mathrm{ref}}$. Source issues
noted: the cross-term sign of Eq.~(2); Eqs.~(4)--(5) without $1/T$ (irrelevant to the eigenbasis); the
missing $\sqrt3$ of Eq.~(B.5); $N_A$ undefined (life part not implemented).
