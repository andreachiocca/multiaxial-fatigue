% registry: SWTD
\methodhead{SWTD}{Kujawski (2014): deviatoric version of the SWT parameter}\label{sheet:SWTD}

\block{Source} D.~Kujawski, \emph{Int.\ J.\ Fatigue} 67 (2014) 95--102 \cite{kujawski2014},
Eqs.~(23)--(25), p.~7; Tables~2 and~3.

\block{Idea and scope} The normal stress and strain of the SWT parameter are replaced by deviatoric
stresses and principal deviatoric strain amplitudes, so that an energy-type parameter remains
active under compressive mean stresses, where $\sigma_{n,\max}$ of SWT drops to zero. Proposed for
uniaxial (including compression--compression) and torsional cycles; the paper gives no procedure
for non-proportional loading.

\block{Formulation} Kujawski (Eq.~25):
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{SWTD} = \max_{i\in\{1,2,3\}}\ \max\bigl(S_{\max,i}\,\lvert e_{a,i}\rvert,\ S_{\min,i}\,\lvert e_{a,i}\rvert\bigr) ,
\label{eq:swtd-main}
\end{equation}
with $S_{\max,i}$, $S_{\min,i}$ and $e_{a,i}$ taken along the same principal axes, as Eqs.~(23)--(24)
are written in one frame. The repository diagonalises $\mathbf e_a$ once and resolves the deviatoric
stresses on its principal directions,
\begin{equation}
S_{\max,i} = \mathbf v_i^{\mathsf T}\mathbf S_{\max}\mathbf v_i ,\qquad
S_{\min,i} = \mathbf v_i^{\mathsf T}\mathbf S_{\min}\mathbf v_i ,
\label{eq:swtd-frame}
\end{equation}
and negative products count as zero. The effective Poisson's ratio used by the paper for its
Table~2 is $\nu_\text{eff}=0.5-(0.5-\nu)\,\Delta\sigma_\text{eq}/(E\,\Delta\varepsilon_\text{eq})$
(p.~6). In fully reversed torsion Eq.~\eqref{eq:swtd-main} gives $\tau_a\gamma_a/2$.

This is not a critical-plane method: no plane search, tie rule or \texttt{np\_shear\_rule}
applies, and the global shear-amplitude selection does not change it. Non-proportional histories
are handled by the chord rule in Repository choices.

\block{Constants and calibration} None in the paper or in the repository. Only the master-curve
constants are fitted.

\block{Life} The repository uses the master curve on SWTD, Eq.~\eqref{eq:mastercurve}, fitted on
the uniaxial $R=-1$ design set. The guide records no life law of the paper.

\block{Repository choices}
\begin{itemize}
\item Shared eigenframe: $\mathbf S_{\max}$ and $\mathbf S_{\min}$ are projected on the principal
  axes of $\mathbf e_a$, Eq.~\eqref{eq:swtd-frame}, as Eqs.~(23)--(24) require. Sorting the
  three spectra independently (the archived code) doubles the compressive transverse terms of
  Table~2.
\item Non-proportional single harmonics (no procedure in the paper): the states
  $\Sm\pm\mathbf A(\psi)$ (and likewise for strain) are evaluated for 360 phases
  $\psi\in[0,\pi)$ and the largest Eq.~\eqref{eq:swtd-main} is kept. For proportional or in-phase
  cycles this equals the two-step value.
\item Pure torsion: Eq.~\eqref{eq:swtd-main} gives $\tau_a\gamma_a/2$, whereas the paper's Table~3
  (path~A) prints $\tau_a\gamma_a/\sqrt3$; the published equation is kept, without a $2/\sqrt3$
  rescaling.
\item Elastic workbook strains (with $\nu$, not $\nu_\text{eff}$), unless total strains are imported.
\end{itemize}

\block{Worked example} Al~2024-T351, $E=71\,700\MPa$, $\nu=0.3$, elastic strains, stresses
$\sigma_{xx}$, $\tau_{xy}$. Two-step cycle from $(300,120)$ to $(-100,-120)\MPa$:
$\mathrm{SWTD}=0.8186\MPa$. Single harmonic $\sigma_{xx}=250\sin\omega t$,
$\tau_{xy}=150\cos\omega t$ ($90^\circ$ out of phase): $\mathrm{SWTD}=0.5110\MPa$.
