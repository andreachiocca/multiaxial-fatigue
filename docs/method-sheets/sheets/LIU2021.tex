\methodhead{LIU2021}{Liu, Ran, Wei and Zhang (2021): modified shear strain amplitude with material sensitivity}\label{sheet:LIU2021}

\block{Source} Liu, Ran, Wei, Zhang, \emph{Int.\ J.\ Press.\ Vessels Pip.} 194 (2021) 104532
\cite{liu2021}, Eqs.~(18), (19), (23), (27); Table~2.

\block{Idea and scope} Strain-based critical-plane criterion of the TNP family. The maximum
engineering shear strain amplitude is modified by a normal-stress and normal-strain interaction
term scaled by $G$ and the shear fatigue strength coefficient $\tau_f'$, which replaces the
empirical fitting constant of earlier models by material constants. The mean stress enters through
the tensile peak normal stress. Proposed for multiaxial fatigue life prediction of metals under
proportional and non-proportional loading.

\block{Formulation} With the material sensitivity factor (Eq.~23) in Eq.~(19),
\begin{equation}
k'=\frac{\sqrt{\sigma_{n,\max}\,E\,\Delta\varepsilon}}{2\,G\,\Delta\gamma_{\max}},\qquad
\frac{\gamma^*_{\max}}{2}=\frac{\Delta\gamma_{\max}}{2}\left(1+k'\,\frac{\sigma_{n,\max}}{\tau_f'}\right),
\label{eq:liu21-k}
\end{equation}
the modified shear strain amplitude on the maximum shear strain plane is (Eq.~27)
\begin{equation}
\frac{\gamma^*_{\max}}{2}=\frac{\Delta\gamma_{\max}}{2}
\left(1+\frac{\sigma_{n,\max}\sqrt{\sigma_{n,\max}\,E\,\Delta\varepsilon}}{2\,G\,\Delta\gamma_{\max}\,\tau_f'}\right).
\label{eq:liu21-main}
\end{equation}
The repository evaluates the expanded product on every candidate plane:
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{DP}=\frac{\Delta\gamma}{2}+\frac{\sigma_{n,\max}\sqrt{\max(\sigma_{n,\max}\,E\,\Delta\varepsilon_n,\,0)}}{4\,G\,\tau_f'},
\label{eq:liu21-dp}
\end{equation}
with $\Delta\gamma=2\lVert(\Delta\varepsilon_{xz},\Delta\varepsilon_{yz})\rVert$,
$\Delta\varepsilon_n=\lvert\Delta\varepsilon_{zz}\rvert$ and
$\sigma_{n,\max}=\max(\sigma_{zz,0},\sigma_{zz,1},0)$. \texttt{LIU2021} takes the plane of largest
$\Delta\gamma$ (ties to the larger DP); \texttt{LIU2021\_ext} the largest DP over all planes
(Section~\ref{sec:planes}).

\block{Constants and calibration} No fitted criterion constant. In the paper $\tau_f'$ comes from
the torsional fatigue properties, or from $\sigma_f'/\sqrt3$ (Eq.~18). In the repository $\tau_f'$
is the workbook or \texttt{--constants} value; otherwise it is estimated from $S_u$ (see Repository
choices). The master-curve constants $A$, $b$ are fitted on the uniaxial $R=-1$ design tests.

\block{Life} The paper maps $\gamma^*_{\max}/2$ through the torsional strain--life curve. The
repository uses the master curve on DP (Section~\ref{sec:mastercurve}).

\block{Repository choices}
\begin{itemize}
\item The unsubscripted $\Delta\varepsilon$ of Eqs.~(23) and (27) is read as the normal strain range
  $\Delta\varepsilon_n$ on the candidate plane (unresolved interpretation).
\item Expanding the outer $\Delta\gamma_{\max}/2$, Eq.~\eqref{eq:liu21-dp}, keeps DP finite when
  $\Delta\gamma=0$.
\item $\sigma_{n,\max}<0$ is clipped to 0.
\item $\tau_f'$ is optional; when missing, the uniform material law $\sigma_f'=1.5\,S_u$ and von
  Mises $\tau_f'=\sigma_f'/\sqrt3$ give it, with a warning. Fitted Basquin constants of the
  pipeline are not passed to this model, so the documented fallback is kept.
\item Out-of-phase shear range: longest chord (\texttt{np\_shear\_rule = "longest\_chord"}).
\item Linear-elastic strains; life from the master curve. \texttt{LIU2021\_ext} is a repository
  variant.
\end{itemize}

\block{Worked example} GH4169 constants of Table~2: $E=182$\,GPa, $\nu=0.325$, $S_u=1400\MPa$,
$\tau_f'=1565.2/\sqrt3\MPa$, with linear-elastic strains (a demonstration, not a life validation).
Reversed tension $\sigma_a=400\MPa$ on the $45^\circ$ plane
($\Delta\gamma=2(1+\nu)\sigma_a/E$, $\Delta\varepsilon_n=(1-\nu)\sigma_a/E$,
$\sigma_{n,\max}=\sigma_a/2$): DP $=0.003099$ (\texttt{LIU2021\_ext} $0.003144$). Reversed torsion
$\tau_a=250\MPa$: $\sigma_{n,\max}=0$, DP $=\tau_a/G=0.003640$.

\block{Verification} Checked: Eq.~\eqref{eq:liu21-main} equals Eq.~(19) with $k'$ of Eq.~(23); the
zero normal-stress and the removable zero-shear limits; the $\tau_f'$ fallback warning; the
closed-form plane value. Paper inconsistencies recorded but not used by the code: Eq.~(3) prints
$\tfrac{\tau_{xy}}{2}\sin2\theta$ (the standard transform has $\tau_{xy}\sin2\theta$); Eq.~(6)
writes the range $\Delta\gamma$ with the loading amplitude $\varepsilon_a$ in front, half of
Li et al.\ (2011) Eq.~(15). Not reproduced: the experimental lives of the paper.
