% registry: GSA
\methodhead{GSA}{Ince and Glinka (2014): generalized strain amplitude}\label{sheet:GSA}

\block{Source} A.~Ince, G.~Glinka, \emph{Int.\ J.\ Fatigue} 62 (2014) 34--41 \cite{ince2014},
Eqs.~(1), (5).

\block{Idea and scope} Strain-based critical-plane criterion: the strain-amplitude form of the
generalized strain energy (GSE, Eq.~1). The energy is divided by the fatigue strength coefficients:
each elastic strain amplitude is weighted by the ratio of the maximum stress to its fatigue
strength coefficient, while the plastic strain amplitudes enter unweighted. The parameter is thus
a strain amplitude that can be compared with a strain--life curve. Proposed for proportional and
non-proportional loading, with mean stresses.

\block{Formulation} Eq.~(5), maximised over the planes (the plane rule is the parameter itself):
\begin{equation}
\frac{\Delta\varepsilon^*}{2}=\left[\frac{\tau_{\max}}{\tau_f'}\frac{\Delta\gamma_e}{2}
+\frac{\Delta\gamma_p}{2}+\frac{\sigma_{n,\max}}{\sigma_f'}\frac{\Delta\varepsilon_e}{2}
+\frac{\Delta\varepsilon_p}{2}\right]_{\max} .
\label{eq:gsa-main}
\end{equation}
The repository evaluates the bracket on every candidate plane with the strain decomposition
$\Teps_p=\Teps-\Teps_e(\Tsig;E,\nu)$, applied to the harmonic tensors before the ranges are taken.
With elastic workbook strains the plastic terms vanish and, with the default $\tau_f'$,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{DP}=\frac{1}{\sigma_f'}\left(\sqrt3\,\tau_{\max}\gamma_a+\sigma_{n,\max}\varepsilon_{n,a}\right).
\label{eq:gsa-elastic}
\end{equation}
Fully reversed tension then gives, with $a=2\sqrt3(1+\nu)-\nu$,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{DP}(\theta)=\frac{\sigma_a^2}{E\sigma_f'}\left[a\,c-(a-1)\,c^2\right],\qquad
\mathrm{DP}_{\max}=\frac{\sigma_a^2}{E\sigma_f'}\,\frac{a^2}{4(a-1)},
\label{eq:gsa-uni}
\end{equation}
and fully reversed torsion $\mathrm{DP}=2\sqrt3(1+\nu)\tau_a^2/(E\sigma_f')$. Equal damage at the
two limits implies $\tm/\fm\approx0.55$ for $\nu=0.3$.

\block{Constants and calibration} No fitted criterion constant. $\sigma_f'$ and $\tau_f'$ are read
from the workbook or estimated, never fitted by the pipeline. Only the master-curve constants $A$,
$b$ are fitted on the uniaxial $R=-1$ design tests.

\block{Life} The paper relates the parameter to a strain--life curve. The repository uses the
master curve on DP (Section~\ref{sec:mastercurve}). DP is quadratic in the load, so the
load-equivalent error (Eq.~\eqref{eq:eload}) is the scale-free comparison metric.

\block{Repository choices}
\begin{itemize}
\item $\sigma_{n,\max}<0$ is clipped to 0.
\item Missing $\sigma_f'$: uniform material law $\sigma_f'=1.5\,S_u$ (Zhao et al., 2020), a steel
  relation, with a warning. Missing $\tau_f'$: $\tau_f'=\sigma_f'/\sqrt3$ (von Mises); the paper
  gives no value.
\item With elastic strains and the default $\tau_f'$, $\sigma_f'$ only scales DP, and the fitted
  master curve absorbs that scale; a separately supplied $\tau_f'$ does change the lives.
\item The shear ranges are taken by the default rule \texttt{np\_shear\_rule = "rss"};
  $\tau_{\max}$ is obtained from 720 time samples.
\item Life from the master curve instead of the strain--life curve. The \texttt{\_ext} value is
  identical to \texttt{GSA}.
\end{itemize}

\block{Worked example} Synthetic material (not experimental data): $E=200$\,GPa, $\nu=0.3$,
$\sigma_f'=1350\MPa$, default $\tau_f'$. Reversed tension $\sigma_a=300\MPa$:
DP $=4.5963\cdot10^{-4}$, equal to $\mathrm{DP}_{\max}$ of Eq.~\eqref{eq:gsa-uni}. Reversed torsion
$\tau_a=200\MPa$: DP $=6.6716\cdot10^{-4}$. Equal DP gives $\tm/\fm=0.553$. Doubling $\sigma_f'$
halves DP.

\block{Verification} Checked: the four terms of Eq.~\eqref{eq:gsa-main} on a plane state with
elastic and plastic strains, and the default $\tau_f'=\sigma_f'/\sqrt3$. Not reproduced: the
experimental lives of the paper.
