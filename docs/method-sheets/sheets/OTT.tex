% registry: OTT
\methodhead{OTT}{Tveit, Reyes and Erduran (2024): reduced Ottosen criterion with a Hershey--Hosford norm}\label{sheet:OTT}

\block{Source} Tveit, Reyes and Erduran, \emph{Results in Engineering} 22 (2024) 102171
\cite{tveit2024}, Eqs.~(11)--(17) and~(19), p.~6; Table~A.1. The reduced form casts the continuum
endurance surface of Ottosen, Stenstr\"om and Ristinmaa \cite{ottosen2008} as a cycle-based
criterion.

\block{Idea and scope} An endurance condition on two cycle quantities: the amplitude of a
non-quadratic (Hershey--Hosford) deviatoric norm and the mean of the first stress invariant. The
exponent $m$ of the norm is set by the limit ratio $t_{-1}/b_{-1}$, so the deviatoric surface moves
between Tresca ($m=1$) and von Mises ($m=2$) and beyond. The mean term is calibrated on the repeated
($R=0$) bending strength. Proposed for the fatigue limit of ductile metals under proportional and
non-proportional loading with mean stresses; the paper's calibrated range is
$0.5\le t_{-1}/b_{-1}\le0.5852$.

\block{Formulation} Fatigue endurance requires (Eq.~11)
\begin{equation}
\sigma_{e,a} + A\,I_{1,m} \le S_0 ,
\label{eq:ott-main}
\end{equation}
with the mean first invariant (Eq.~12) and, for proportional cycles, the Hershey--Hosford effective
stress amplitude of exponent $m$ (Eq.~13),
\begin{gather}
I_{1,m} = \tfrac12\Bigl(\min_P I_1 + \max_P I_1\Bigr) , \label{eq:ott-i1m}\\
\sigma_{e,a} = \left(\frac{|s_{1,a}-s_{2,a}|^m + |s_{2,a}-s_{3,a}|^m + |s_{3,a}-s_{1,a}|^m}{2}\right)^{1/m} .
\label{eq:ott-se}
\end{gather}
The three material parameters follow from Eqs.~(14)--(17):
\begin{gather}
S_0 = b_{-1}, \qquad A = \frac{2\,b_{-1}}{b_0} - 1 , \label{eq:ott-const}\\
\kappa_{\mathrm{T}} = \frac{t_{-1}}{b_{-1}} = \left(1 + 2^{m-1}\right)^{-1/m} . \label{eq:ott-kappa}
\end{gather}
For $m=2$, $\sigma_{e,a}$ is the von Mises amplitude and $\kappa_{\mathrm{T}}=1/\sqrt3$; $m=1$ gives
Tresca and $\kappa_{\mathrm{T}}=1/2$. Eq.~\eqref{eq:ott-kappa} reaches its maximum
$\kappa_{\mathrm{T}}\approx0.58524$ at $m=2.7670$. For non-proportional cycles the paper calls for
the smallest hypersphere that encloses the deviatoric path, without giving a construction. The
paper's error index is $\mathrm{Err}=(1-f_a)\,100\,\%$ (Eq.~19), with $f_a$ the amplitude scale
factor to the endurance limit.

\block{Constants and calibration} Paper: $S_0$, $A$ and $m$ in closed form from $b_{-1}$, $t_{-1}$
and $b_0$ (Eqs.~14--17); nothing is fitted. Repository: the same closed forms. $m$ is read from
\texttt{m\_OTT} when supplied; otherwise Eq.~\eqref{eq:ott-kappa} is inverted by bisection on
$1\le m\le2.7670$. $A$ is read from \texttt{A\_OTT} when supplied; otherwise from $b_0$
(\texttt{Sig0}), or from the estimate of Eq.~(1) of B\"ohme, Papuga and Lange \cite{bohme2026} built
from $S_u$ and $\fm$. Only the master-curve constants are fitted.

\block{Life} No native life law. The left-hand side of Eq.~\eqref{eq:ott-main}, in MPa, is the
damage parameter of the shared master curve (Section~\ref{sec:mastercurve}), a repository
extension of an endurance criterion.

\block{Repository choices}
\begin{itemize}
\item The whole left-hand side $\sigma_{e,a}+A\,I_{1,m}$ is the damage parameter (MPa).
\item $b_0$: from \texttt{A\_OTT} when supplied, otherwise from \texttt{Sig0}, otherwise from the
  B\"ohme et al.\ (2026) Eq.~(1) estimate built from \texttt{Su} and \texttt{Sigm1}.
\item $m$: when \texttt{m\_OTT} is omitted, Eq.~\eqref{eq:ott-kappa} is inverted for
  $\kappa_{\mathrm{T}}=\tm/\fm$ and clamped to $[1.0,\,2.7670]$: $\kappa_{\mathrm{T}}\le0.5$ gives
  $m=1$, and $\kappa_{\mathrm{T}}\ge0.58524$ gives the cap $m=2.7670$ (outside the calibrated range).
\item Non-proportional cycles: the Hershey--Hosford norm is centrally symmetric and a single-harmonic
  path is symmetric about $\Sm$, so the enclosing ball is centred at $\Sm$ and its radius is the
  maximum of $\sigma_e\bigl(\Tsig(t)-\Sm\bigr)$ over 720 phase angles of one period. For proportional
  cycles this recovers Eq.~\eqref{eq:ott-se} exactly.
\end{itemize}

\block{Worked example} Steel with $b_{-1}=433$, $t_{-1}=250$, $b_0=700\MPa$: $m=2.0007$. Bending
amplitude $300\MPa$ with mean $100\MPa$ and torsion amplitude $150\MPa$: in phase,
$\sigma_{e,a}+A\,I_{1,m}=420.57\MPa$; at $90^\circ$, $323.71\MPa$.

\block{Verification} Table~A.1: every in-phase row reproduces the printed $\mathrm{Err}$ for both the
modified ($m$) and the original ($m=2$) model. The out-of-phase rows of the table come from the
paper's incremental (Runge--Kutta) model; the reduced closed form agrees with them within about
0.8 points, except 30NCD16 at $90^\circ$, where the table prints 0.00 for both models and the reduced
form gives $-26$ to $-34\,\%$ (strict xfail).
