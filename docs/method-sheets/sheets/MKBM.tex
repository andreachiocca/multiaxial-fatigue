% registry: MKBM
\methodhead{MKBM}{Li, Zhang, Sun and Li (2011): modified Kandil--Brown--Miller parameter}\label{sheet:MKBM}

\block{Source} J.~Li, Z.~Zhang, Q.~Sun, C.~Li, \emph{Int.\ J.\ Fatigue} 33 (2011) 90--101
\cite{li2011}, Eqs.~(7)--(8), (13), (15)--(16), (19), (27).

\block{Idea and scope} Strain-based critical-plane criterion. The Kandil--Brown--Miller parameter
combines the maximum shear strain and the normal strain on the same plane. Li et al.\ multiply the
normal-strain term by $1+\sigma_{n,\max}/\sigma_y$, so that the additional hardening and the crack
opening produced by non-proportional loading also enter the parameter. Proposed for low-cycle
multiaxial fatigue of steels, aluminium and titanium alloys under proportional and out-of-phase
tension--torsion.

\block{Formulation} On the plane of maximum shear strain range (Eq.~27),
\begin{equation}
\frac{\Delta\varepsilon^*_{eq}}{2}=\frac{\Delta\gamma_{\max}}{2}
+\left(1+\frac{\sigma_{n,\max}}{\sigma_y}\right)\frac{\Delta\varepsilon_n}{2} .
\label{eq:mkbm-main}
\end{equation}
For general paths the shear range is the longest chord of the shear-strain path on the plane
(Eq.~19). The repository evaluates on every candidate plane
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{DP}=\gamma_a+\left(1+\frac{\sigma_{n,\max}}{S_y}\right)\varepsilon_{n,a},\qquad
\sigma_{n,\max}=\max(\sigma_{n,m}+\sigma_{n,a},\,0).
\label{eq:mkbm-dp}
\end{equation}
\texttt{MKBM} takes the plane of largest $\Delta\gamma$ (the source's rule; among tied planes the
larger DP); \texttt{MKBM\_ext} the largest DP over all planes (Section~\ref{sec:planes}). With
elastic strains and no mean stress, reversed tension on the $45^\circ$ plane and reversed torsion
give
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{DP}_\text{ten}=(1+\nu)\frac{\sigma_a}{E}+\Bigl(1+\frac{\sigma_a}{2S_y}\Bigr)\frac{(1-\nu)\sigma_a}{2E},\qquad
\mathrm{DP}_\text{tor}=\frac{2(1+\nu)\tau_a}{E} ,
\label{eq:mkbm-limits}
\end{equation}
so the implied ratio of the torsion and tension limits depends on $\sigma_a/S_y$.

\block{Constants and calibration} No criterion-specific constant. In the paper the strain--life
constants of the material give the life; in the repository only the master-curve constants $A$,
$b$ are fitted on the uniaxial $R=-1$ design tests.

\block{Life} The paper relates $\Delta\varepsilon^*_{eq}/2$ to life through the uniaxial
strain--life curve. The repository uses the master curve on DP (Section~\ref{sec:mastercurve}). DP
is not homogeneous in the load, so the load-equivalent error (Eq.~\eqref{eq:eload}) is the
scale-free comparison metric.

\block{Repository choices}
\begin{itemize}
\item $\sigma_{n,\max}<0$ is clipped to 0, so compression never lowers the factor below 1.
\item The workbook monotonic yield strength $S_y$ replaces $\sigma_y$.
\item Strains are linear-elastic from $E$ and $\nu$; the cyclic plasticity and additional hardening
  that motivate the correction are not simulated.
\item Out-of-phase shear range: longest chord of the shear-strain path
  (\texttt{np\_shear\_rule = "longest\_chord"}), as in Eq.~(19).
\item Life from the master curve instead of the strain--life curve.
\item \texttt{MKBM\_ext} is a repository variant.
\end{itemize}

\block{Worked example} Synthetic material (not experimental data): $E=200$\,GPa, $\nu=0.3$,
$S_y=400\MPa$. Reversed tension $\sigma_a=300\MPa$: MKBM $=0.002672$ (\texttt{MKBM\_ext}
$0.003372$, on a plane closer to the load axis); reversed torsion $\tau_a=200\MPa$: $0.002600$; both
equal Eq.~\eqref{eq:mkbm-limits}. Equal DP gives $\tm/\fm=0.685$ for $\fm=300\MPa$. At $90^\circ$
out of phase with $\sigma_a=200$, $\tau_a=100\MPa$: $0.002800$ for both variants. Every plane
containing the radial direction has the same shear range; the tie goes to the plane normal to the
axis, where $\varepsilon_{n,a}=\sigma_a/E$ and $\sigma_{n,\max}=\sigma_a$.

\block{Verification} Checked: the thin-walled tube plane strains, Eqs.~(7)--(8), at every instant;
the harmonic evaluator against Eqs.~(15)--(16); the critical-plane angle of Eq.~(13); the
normal-stress factor of Eq.~\eqref{eq:mkbm-main}, with the clipping of compression; the
longest-chord shear range of Eq.~(19). The paper swaps the mean-strain terms of Eqs.~(9) and (10);
Eqs.~(15)--(16) are consistent with Eqs.~(7)--(8). Not reproduced: the experimental lives.
