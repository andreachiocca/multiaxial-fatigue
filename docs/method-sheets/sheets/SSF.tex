% registry: SSF
\methodhead{SSF}{Anes, Reis, Li, Fonte and de Freitas (2014): stress scale factor}\label{sheet:SSF}

\block{Source} V.~Anes, L.~Reis, B.~Li, M.~Fonte, M.~de Freitas, \emph{Int.\ J.\ Fatigue} 62 (2014)
21--33 \cite{anes2014}, Eqs.~(29)--(31); Tables~4--10, Section~4.5, Figs.~12--14.

\block{Idea and scope} Criteria such as Crossland, Matake or Dang Van weight the axial stress against
the shear stress with a constant derived from the fatigue limits. Anes et al.\ measure this ``stress
scale factor'' (ssf) on proportional tension--torsion tests, find that it depends on the axial stress
level and on the amplitude ratio, and fit an ssf surface. Along a non-proportional path the
instantaneous ssf turns each instant of the axial stress into an equivalent shear stress, compared
with the pure-shear S--N curve. Proposed for finite-life tension--torsion fatigue of high-strength
42CrMo4; mean stresses are not addressed (all tests at $R=-1$).

\block{Inputs}
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}llp{100mm}@{}}
\toprule
Symbol & Unit & Meaning\\\midrule
$\sigma(t)$, $\tau(t)$ & MPa & axial normal stress and shear stress acting in the same plane (tension--torsion)\\
$\sigma_a$, $\tau_a$ & MPa & their amplitudes\\
$\lambda$ & rad & load angle, Eq.~\eqref{eq:ssf-lambda}\\
$\mathrm{ssf}(\sigma,\lambda)$ & -- & stress scale factor, Eq.~\eqref{eq:ssf-surface}\\

$f$, $g$, $h$, $i$ & rad$^{-2}$, \dots, rad$^{-5}$ & angle coefficients of the ssf surface (no $e$ term)\\
$\tau_{eqv}$ & MPa & equivalent shear stress, Eq.~\eqref{eq:ssf-main}\\
$N_f$ & cycles & life\\
$AB$, $AC$, $AD$ & MPa & S--N trend lines at a given life: shear stress of a case, pure-shear reference, axial stress of the case; $BC=AC-AB$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\block{Formulation} Eqs.~(29)--(31):
\begin{gather}
\tau_{eqv} = \max_t\Bigl[\tau(t) + \mathrm{ssf}(\lambda,\sigma)\,\sigma(t)\Bigr] , \label{eq:ssf-main}\\
\lambda = \tan^{-1}\frac{\tau}{\sigma} , \label{eq:ssf-lambda}\\
\mathrm{ssf}(\sigma,\lambda) = a + b\sigma + c\sigma^2 + d\sigma^3 + f\lambda^2 + g\lambda^3 + h\lambda^4 + i\lambda^5 ,
\label{eq:ssf-surface}
\end{gather}
with, for the paper's 42CrMo4, $a=2.69$, $b=-9.90\times10^{-3}$, $c=1.69\times10^{-5}$,
$d=-9.52\times10^{-9}$, $f=-5.99$, $g=11.72$, $h=-8.04$, $i=1.63$ ($\sigma$ in MPa, $\lambda$ in rad).
Under non-proportional loading the instantaneous stresses enter Eq.~\eqref{eq:ssf-surface}, so
$\lambda(t)$ and $\mathrm{ssf}(t)$ vary along the cycle (Section~4.5).

\block{Constants and calibration} Paper (Tables~4--9): for each proportional case, axial ($AD$) and shear
($AB$) S--N trend lines are fitted, together with the pure-shear reference ($AC$, case~2:
$\tau_a=864.78\,N_f^{-0.061}$). At each life $\mathrm{ssf}=(AC-AB)/AD$, the shear increment needed to
reach the pure-shear curve per unit axial stress, and Eq.~(31) is regressed on these points
($R^2=0.98$). This needs proportional tension--torsion S--N data at several amplitude ratios plus pure
tension and pure torsion (cases~1--5). Repository: only the published surface (maintainer decision).
The eight coefficients are required constants \texttt{a\_SSF}, \dots, \texttt{i\_SSF}, without them the
model is skipped; the paper's values are in the published-constants record
\texttt{Calibrations/42CrMo4\_Reis/SSF\_published.json}, read by \texttt{--constants}. No per-material
recalibration and no refit. The workbook 42CrMo4\_Reis holds the tests the surface was fitted on, so
its SSF results are in-sample (the paper's case~6 is the only test not used in the fit).

\block{Life} No native life law in the repository. In the paper $\tau_{eqv}$ is read on the pure-shear
S--N curve; the repository fits the shared master curve (Section~\ref{sec:mastercurve}) with
$\mathrm{DP}=\tau_{eqv}$ on the pure torsion $R=-1$ failures (design set: torsion), a power law as in
the paper.

\block{Repository choices}
\begin{itemize}
\item Published surface only, with the coefficients as printed (maintainer decisions); they miss the
  paper's Table~9 by up to 0.06.
\item Magnitudes: $\lambda=\tan^{-1}(|\tau|/|\sigma|)\in[0,\pi/2]$ and
  $\tau_{eqv}=\max_t\bigl[|\tau(t)|+\mathrm{ssf}(|\sigma(t)|,\lambda(t))\,|\sigma(t)|\bigr]$, so the result
  does not depend on axis sign conventions; it equals the signed form of Fig.~14 wherever $\tau$ and
  $\sigma$ share the sign.
\item The cycle is sampled at 4096 points.
\item Scope: fully reversed loading with one normal and one shear stress in the same plane; anything
  else raises \texttt{LoadingScopeError}.
\item A warning when $|\sigma|$ exceeds $737\MPa$, the largest axial amplitude behind the fit.
\item Life: master curve on the pure torsion failures (\texttt{design\_loading = "torsion"}).
\item Cross-batch use: the surface may be applied to another batch of the same steel only through a
  transfer with 42CrMo4\_Reis as the design batch; results are then out of sample.
\end{itemize}

\block{Worked example} Published coefficients. Proportional case~4 at $10^5$ cycles (Table~7),
$\sigma_a=480$, $\tau_a=277\MPa$: $\lambda=0.52$~rad, $\mathrm{ssf}=0.279$ (Table~7: 0.32),
$\tau_{eqv}=411.0\MPa$ (pure-shear reference $428\MPa$). Non-proportional case~6, $\sigma_a=510$,
$\tau_a=294\MPa$ at $90^\circ$: $\tau_{eqv}=415.3\MPa$ (text, p.~31: $434\MPa$). Both gaps come from
the rounding of the printed coefficients.

\block{Verification} Tables~5--8 are reproduced from the Table~4 trend lines (the calibration
procedure). Two printed cells are misprints and strict xfails: Table~5 prints $BC=0$ for case~1, where
it should be $AC-AB=AC$; Table~8 prints $AC=508$ at $10^3$ cycles, where it should be 567; in both, the
table's own ssf column uses the correct value. Table~9: Eq.~(31) as printed matches within 0.06 (RMS
gap below 0.035). Case~6: the ssf history spans 0.37--0.79 as in Fig.~13; the peak $\tau_{eqv}$ is
$415\MPa$ against the printed 434, and matching within 1\,\% is a strict xfail. Also checked:
proportional peaks, pure torsion as the reference, the scope errors and the published-constants
record.
