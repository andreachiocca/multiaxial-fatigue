\methodhead{MOREL}{Morel (1998): mesoscopic life prediction under constant amplitude}\label{sheet:MOREL}

\block{Source} F.~Morel, \emph{Fatigue Fract.\ Eng.\ Mater.\ Struct.}\ 21 (1998) 241--256
\cite{morel1998}, Eqs.~(9)--(11), (33)--(38); worked example pp.~252--253. The generalised shear
amplitude is that of Papadopoulos \cite{papadopoulos2001}.

\block{Idea and scope} Mesoscopic (grain-scale plasticity) model with a native life law. Fatigue damage
is the plastic slip accumulated in the worst-oriented grains. A fatigue limit follows from
Papadopoulos' generalised shear amplitude and the hydrostatic stress; the life above the limit follows
from a three-parameter law in the critical-plane shear amplitude, which accounts for the shape of the
load path through a phase-difference coefficient. Proposed for constant-amplitude multiaxial
high-cycle fatigue, including out-of-phase loading.

\block{Formulation} Endurance criterion (Eqs.~9--11):
\begin{gather}
T_S + a\,P_{\max} \le b , \qquad
T_S = \max_{\n}\sqrt{\int_0^{2\pi}\tau_a(\n,\psi)^2\,d\psi} = \sqrt\pi\,T_a , \label{eq:morel-limit}\\
a = \frac{\sqrt\pi\,(\tm - \fm/2)}{\fm/3} , \qquad b = \sqrt\pi\,\tm . \label{eq:morel-ab}
\end{gather}
For the similar-loading group, with $f=T_S/P_a$, the limit loading is (Eq.~33)
\begin{equation}
T_{S,\lim} = \frac{f\,(b - a\,P_m)}{a + f} .
\label{eq:morel-tslim}
\end{equation}
The phase-difference coefficient is $H=T_S/C_A$ (Eq.~35); $H=\sqrt\pi$ for proportional and $\sqrt{2\pi}$
for circular paths. The limit shear amplitude is $\tau_{\lim}=T_{S,\lim}/H$ (Eq.~37), and the life is
(Eq.~38)
\begin{equation}
N_i = p\,\ln\frac{C_A}{C_A-\tau_{\lim}} + q\,\frac{\tau_{\lim}}{C_A-\tau_{\lim}} - \frac{r}{C_A} ,
\qquad C_A > \tau_{\lim}\quad(\text{else } N_i=\infty) .
\label{eq:morel-life}
\end{equation}

\block{Constants and calibration} Paper: $a$, $b$ from the two fatigue limits (Eq.~10); $p$, $q$, $r$
fitted to one S--N curve, without a stated objective and without printed values. Repository: $a$, $b$ as
in Eq.~(10); $p$, $q$, $r$ fitted on the uniaxial tension $R=-1$ failures above the Morel limit (at least
three), by linear least squares on the relative life error (Eq.~\eqref{eq:morel-life} is linear in $p$,
$q$, $r$), and recorded with the run. These tests are design rows.

\block{Life} Native life law, Eq.~\eqref{eq:morel-life} with the fitted $p$, $q$, $r$
(\texttt{life\_prediction = "native"}). Tests below the limit get $N_i=\infty$, which is kept and
counted. With fewer than three design tests above the limit there is no native prediction, and it is
not replaced by the master curve. An empirical master curve on $\mathrm{DP}=T_S/T_{S,\lim}$ is also
fitted for the damage-parameter comparison; it does not assess Eq.~(38).

\block{Repository choices}
\begin{itemize}
\item Fitting objective for $p$, $q$, $r$: relative life error, at least three design tests above the
  limit (the paper states no objective).
\item Damage parameter $\mathrm{DP}=T_S/T_{S,\lim}$, which equals the Papadopoulos (2001) Eq.~(41) index
  exactly (same iso-life planes).
\item For one harmonic, $T_s^2=\pi(|\mathbf t_s|^2+|\mathbf t_c|^2)$ on a plane, with $\mathbf t_s$,
  $\mathbf t_c$ the sine and cosine parts of the shear vector. The critical plane maximises $T_S$ on a
  sphere grid with pattern-search refinement; $C_A$ is the exact semi-major axis of the shear ellipse
  on that plane.
\item Below the limit the native life is infinite and reported as such.
\item Misprint: the worked example prints $b=775\MPa$; Eq.~(10) gives $\sqrt\pi\cdot426=755\MPa$, and only
  755 reproduces the printed $T_{S,\lim}=633\MPa$.
\end{itemize}

\block{Worked example} The paper's example: 30NCD16, $\fm=680$, $\tm=426\MPa$, $\sigma_{11,a}=600\MPa$ and
$\sigma_{12,a}=335\MPa$ at $90^\circ$. Printed: $T_S=697$, $a=0.67$, $T_{S,\lim}=633$, $C_A=282\MPa$,
$H=2.47$. Repository: $a=0.6725$, $b=755.1\MPa$, $T_S=697.5$, $T_{S,\lim}=633.0$, $C_A=285.1\MPa$,
$H=2.447$; index $T_S/T_{S,\lim}=1.1019$, above the limit, hence a finite life. A synthetic check recovers
known $p$, $q$, $r$ from lives generated with Eq.~(38) (relative tolerance $10^{-8}$).

\block{Verification} Checked: the Eq.~(10) parameters and the $b$ misprint; $T_S$ and $T_{S,\lim}$ of the
worked example; $C_A$ printed 282 against 285.1 computed is a strict xfail (unresolved, 1\,\%); the
bounds of $H$; the identity with the Papadopoulos index; Eq.~(38) and the recovery of $p$, $q$, $r$. Not
reproduced: the example's life $N_i=2\cdot10^5$, because $p$, $q$, $r$ are not printed.
