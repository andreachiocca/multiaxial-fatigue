\methodhead{MSWT}{Zhu et al.\ (2018), after Jiang (2000): modified SWT, scalar range form}\label{sheet:MSWT}

\block{Source} S.-P.~Zhu, Z.-Y.~Yu, J.~Correia, A.~De~Jesus, F.~Berto, \emph{Int.\ J.\ Fatigue} 113
(2018) 213--229 \cite{zhu2018}, Eq.~(13), p.~8; Section~4.1, p.~14. Lineage: Y.~Jiang,
\emph{Fatigue Fract.\ Engng Mater.\ Struct.} 23(1) (2000) 19--32 \cite{jiang2000}, Eq.~(6), the
plastic-work increment $dY = a\,\sigma\,d\varepsilon^p + \tfrac{1-a}{2}\,\tau\,d\gamma^p$.

\block{Idea and scope} The scalar range form of Jiang's plastic-work increment, as written by Zhu et
al.: a weighted sum of a normal (SWT-type) and a shear strain-energy term, the weight $a$ selecting
tensile, mixed or shear cracking. Proposed for multiaxial fatigue lives of tensile-, mixed- and
shear-cracking metals. It is not Jiang's incremental cyclic-plasticity criterion.

\block{Formulation} Zhu et al.\ (Eq.~13) write, on the maximum-damage plane,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{DP}_\mathrm{MSWT} = 2\,a\,\Delta\varepsilon\,\langle\sigma_{\max}\rangle
 + \frac{1-a}{2}\,\Delta\gamma\,\Delta\tau .
\label{eq:mswt-main}
\end{equation}
With a plastic Poisson's ratio of 0.5, $a\le0.375$ favours shear planes, $0.375<a<0.5$ mixed
planes and $a\ge0.5$ tensile planes. In amplitudes,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{DP} = 4\,a\,\langle\sigma_{n,\max}\rangle\,\varepsilon_{n,a} + 2(1-a)\,\tau_a\,\gamma_a ,
\qquad \Delta\gamma = 2\lVert(\Delta\varepsilon_{xz},\Delta\varepsilon_{yz})\rVert,\quad
\Delta\tau = \lVert(\Delta\tau_{xz},\Delta\tau_{yz})\rVert .
\label{eq:mswt-plane}
\end{equation}
\emph{Critical plane}: the plane of maximum DP; the selection metric is DP itself, so ties are
immaterial and \texttt{MSWT\_ext} equals \texttt{MSWT}. \emph{Shear amplitude}: default
\texttt{np\_shear\_rule = "rss"}, the MCE amplitude $\sqrt{a_s^2+b_s^2}$ of the elliptical shear
path with semiaxes $a_s$, $b_s$; the longest chord (MCC) can be selected globally
(\texttt{docs/shear-amplitude.md}, ``Selection for every method'').

In fully reversed torsion with elastic strains, the shear plane gives $4(1-a)(1+\nu)\tau_a^2/E$ and
the $45^\circ$ plane $4a(1+\nu)\tau_a^2/E$: the shear plane controls for $a<0.5$.

\block{Constants and calibration} \emph{Paper} (Section~4.1, p.~14): $a$ brings the axial and
torsional DP--life curves as close together as possible. \emph{Repository}
(\texttt{multifat-pipeline}), when $a$ is in neither the workbook nor \texttt{--constants}: minimum
$\ln N_f$ scatter of the $R=-1$ tension and torsion failures around one master curve,
$a\in[0,1]$; $a=1$ is accepted as a physical upper limit. On fatigue-limit datasets $a\in[0,1]$
equalises the axial and torsional limit DP. The tests behind $a$ are design rows
(\texttt{Flag = 0}). In \texttt{multifat-run} without a value, $a=0.5$ with a warning.

\block{Life} The repository uses the master curve on DP, Eq.~\eqref{eq:mastercurve}, fitted on the
uniaxial $R=-1$ design set.

\block{Repository choices}
\begin{itemize}
\item Scalar range form of Eq.~\eqref{eq:mswt-main} on elastic workbook strains, not Jiang's
  incremental cyclic plasticity.
\item Non-proportional shear range by the default \texttt{rss} rule.
\item $\sigma_{n,\max}$ Macaulay-bracketed at zero.
\item $a$ validated finite in $[0,1]$ (an error outside, not a silent clip); default 0.5 with a
  warning when omitted.
\item Weighting correction of 18 October 2026 (finding U2): earlier revisions gave the shear term
  half its intended weight (the module docstring states the former expression,
  $\sigma_{\max}\Delta\varepsilon/2+(1-a)\Delta\gamma\Delta\tau/4$); old calibration records are
  converted by $a'=a/(2-a)$, which yields identical lives.
\item Life from the shared master curve.
\end{itemize}

\block{Worked example} Synthetic material, $E=200\,000\MPa$, $\nu=0.3$, zero mean. Tension
$\sigma_a=300\MPa$ with $a=0.4$: $\mathrm{DP}=0.720000\MPa$ (\texttt{\_ext} identical). Torsion
$\tau_a=200\MPa$: $\mathrm{DP}=0.624000\MPa$ for both $a=0.4$ and $a=0.6$, i.e.\
$4\cdot0.6\,(1+\nu)\tau_a^2/E$.
