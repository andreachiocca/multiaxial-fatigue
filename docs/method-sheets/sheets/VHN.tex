% registry: VHN
\methodhead{VHN}{Vu, Halm and Nadot (2010): stress-invariant criterion for complex loading}\label{sheet:VHN}

\block{Source} Q.~H.~Vu, D.~Halm, Y.~Nadot, \emph{Int.\ J.\ Fatigue} 32 (2010) 1004--1014
\cite{vu2010}, Eqs.~(6)--(21); Tables~1--14.

\block{Idea and scope} Stress-invariant endurance criterion that captures the effect of out-of-phase
loading without an integration over planes. It combines the peak of the alternating octahedral shear
measure, its mean over the cycle (larger for a rotating, non-proportional path) and a hydrostatic
term whose mean-stress weight depends on the ultimate strength class. Proposed for the fatigue limit
of metals under periodic proportional and non-proportional loading, including mean stresses and
loads of different frequencies (Tables~13--14).

\block{Formulation} The criterion requires $f_{\max}=\max_t f(t)\le\beta$ (Eq.~8), with
\begin{gather}
f(t) = \sqrt{\gamma_1\,J'_2(t)^2 + \gamma_2\,J_{2,\mathrm{mean}}^2 + \gamma_3\,I_f(I_{1,a},I_{1,m})} ,
\label{eq:vhn-main}\\
\beta = \tm , \qquad \gamma_3 = \frac{\tm^2 - \fm^2/3}{\fm} , \qquad
\gamma_1 + \frac{4\,\gamma_2}{\pi^2} = 1 \quad\text{(Eq.~13)} .
\label{eq:vhn-const}
\end{gather}
The class constants (Eqs.~17--20) are
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
R_m < 750\MPa :&\quad \gamma_1 = 0.65,\ \gamma_2 = 0.8636,\ I_f = I_{1,a} + I_{1,m} ,\\
R_m > 750\MPa :&\quad \gamma_1 = 0.3,\ \gamma_2 = 1.7272,\ I_f = I_{1,a} + \frac{\fm}{\tm}\,I_{1,m} .
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:vhn-class}
\end{equation}
The paper's error index is $100\,(f_{\max}/\tm-1)$ (Eq.~21).

\block{Constants and calibration} Paper: $\beta$ and $\gamma_3$ from the two fatigue limits; $\gamma_1$
and $\gamma_2$ by strength class, tied by Eq.~(13). Repository: the same closed forms from the workbook
$\fm$, $\tm$ and $S_u$ (all finite and positive); there are no optional constants. Only the
master-curve constants are fitted.

\block{Life} No native life law. The index $f_{\max}/\tm$ is the damage parameter of the shared master
curve (Section~\ref{sec:mastercurve}), a repository extension of a fatigue-limit criterion.

\block{Repository choices}
\begin{itemize}
\item For one harmonic the alternating deviator traces an ellipse with $J'_2$ semi-axes $p\ge q$, so
  $\max_t J'_2=p$ and $J_{2,\mathrm{mean}}=\tfrac{2}{\pi}\,p\,E(1-q^2/p^2)$, computed in closed form
  with the arithmetic--geometric mean; no time discretisation.
\item The class boundary: $S_u<750\MPa$ takes the low-strength constants, $S_u\ge750\MPa$ the
  high-strength ones.
\item A radicand made negative by a strong compressive mean hydrostatic stress is clamped to~0.
\item The returned value is the index $f_{\max}/\tm$.
\item Single-harmonic scope: the two-frequency histories of Tables~13--14 (asynchronous loading) are
  not implemented.
\end{itemize}

\block{Worked example} High-strength steel, $\fm=398$, $\tm=260$, $R_m=1025\MPa$; axial
$\sigma_m=100$, $\sigma_a=260\MPa$ with torsion $\tau_a=150\MPa$ at $90^\circ$: index $0.9500$.
