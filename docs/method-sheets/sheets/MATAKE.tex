% registry: MATAKE
\methodhead{MATAKE}{Matake (1977): fatigue limit on the plane of maximum shear stress amplitude}\label{sheet:MATAKE}

\block{Source} T.~Matake, \emph{Bull.\ JSME} 20 (1977) 257--263 \cite{matake1977}, Eq.~(1), p.~5;
Appendix, p.~7; Table~3. Restated with the circumscribed-circle amplitude by Papadopoulos et al.\
\cite{papadopoulos1997}, Table~1.

\block{Idea and scope} Like Findley, a shear amplitude plus a normal stress on a plane, but the
critical plane is the plane of maximum shear stress amplitude, and the normal-stress coefficient
follows directly from the two fatigue limits. Proposed for the fatigue limit of metals under
combined stresses.

\block{Inputs}
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}llp{100mm}@{}}
\toprule
Symbol & Unit & Meaning\\\midrule
$\tau_a$ & MPa & shear stress amplitude on the plane\\
$\sigma_{n,\max}$ & MPa & maximum normal stress on the plane over the cycle, $\sigma_{n,m}+\sigma_{n,a}$\\
$\sigma_{n,m}$, $\sigma_{n,a}$ & MPa & mean and amplitude of the normal stress on the plane\\
$\Delta\tau$ & MPa & shear stress range on the plane, $\tau_a=\Delta\tau/2$\\
$a\ge b$ & MPa & semi-axes of the elliptical shear path of an out-of-phase cycle\\
$\fm$, $\tm$ & MPa & fully reversed bending (or axial) and torsion fatigue limits\\
$\mu$ & -- & normal stress coefficient, Eq.~\eqref{eq:mtk-mu}\\
$\mathrm{DP}$ & MPa & damage parameter, Eq.~\eqref{eq:mtk-dp}; $\mathrm{DP}=\tm$ on the limit\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\block{Formulation} On the plane of maximum shear stress amplitude (Eq.~1, p.~5),
\begin{equation}
\tau_a + \mu\,\sigma_{n,\max} = \tm ,\qquad \mu = \frac{2\tm}{\fm} - 1 .
\label{eq:mtk-mu}
\end{equation}
The coefficient (Appendix, p.~7) is the slope of the line through $(0,\tm)$ (torsion) and
$(\fm/2,\fm/2)$ (tension, $45^\circ$ plane). The Appendix prints two different, inconsistent
expressions for it; both are misprints of $2\tm/\fm-1$. The repository evaluates on every candidate
plane
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{DP} = \tau_a + \mu\,\sigma_{n,\max},\qquad \sigma_{n,\max}=\max(\sigma_{n,m}+\sigma_{n,a},\,0),
\label{eq:mtk-dp}
\end{equation}
with $\mathrm{DP}=\tm$ at both fully reversed limits. \emph{Critical plane}: \texttt{MATAKE} takes
the plane of largest $\tau_a$ (selection metric $\Delta\tau$), Matake's rule; \texttt{MATAKE\_ext}
takes the largest DP over all planes. \emph{Ties} (e.g.\ the cone of $45^\circ$ planes in tension) go
to the larger DP. \emph{Shear amplitude}: default \texttt{rss}, $\tau_a=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}$ (MCE); the
global selection of \texttt{docs/shear-amplitude.md} can switch it to the longest chord (MCC,
$\tau_a=a$).

\block{Constants and calibration} Paper and repository: $\mu$ in closed form from the two limits
(workbook \texttt{Sigm1}, \texttt{Taum1}). Nothing is fitted except the master-curve constants on the
uniaxial $R=-1$ design tests. $\mu<0$ when $\tm/\fm<1/2$.

\block{Life} The paper gives none; finite lives use the master curve on DP
(Section~\ref{sec:mastercurve}), a repository extension. On fatigue-limit datasets
$\mathrm{DP}/\tm-1$ is Matake's error index.

\block{Repository choices}
\begin{itemize}
\item Compression clipped: $\sigma_{n,\max}<0$ is set to 0; Matake's linear relation is not claimed for
  compressive normal stresses.
\item Out-of-phase shear amplitude: \texttt{rss}. Papadopoulos et al.\ restate Matake with the
  circumscribed-circle amplitude (\texttt{longest\_chord}), with which their Table~1 column is
  reproduced.
\item \texttt{MATAKE\_ext} (largest DP over all planes) is a repository variant.
\item Life from the shared master curve on DP.
\end{itemize}

\block{Worked example} Synthetic material, $\fm=300$, $\tm=200\MPa$, so $\mu=0.3333$: DP at both
limits equals $\tm=200.0\MPa$. Tension $\sigma_a=200$ with mean $\sigma_m=100\MPa$: on the $45^\circ$
planes $\tau_a=\sigma_a/2$ and $\sigma_{n,\max}=(\sigma_m+\sigma_a)/2$, so $\mathrm{DP}=150.00\MPa$,
fatigue index 0.750.

\block{Verification} Matake (1977) Table~3: shear amplitude and normal stress on the maximum-shear
plane reproduced; the coefficient derivation is checked, with the Appendix misprints documented, and
the printed steel row at $22.5^\circ$ is shown to contradict the loading ratio of the grip. Eq.~(1)
passes through both calibration points; the combined-stress limits lie within $\pm10\%$ of Eq.~(1)
(validation, not verification). Papadopoulos et al.\ (1997) Table~1, with the circumscribed-circle
amplitude: 39 of 42 rows within 0.25 points. Strict xfails: row~9 (the closed form gives $+1.7\%$,
the table prints $-1.4\%$: table calculation error) and rows~17 and~28 (mean torsion with phase
shift, unresolved). Out of phase the default \texttt{rss} rule differs from the table (rows 7, 21).
