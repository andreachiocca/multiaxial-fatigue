\methodhead{KUJAWSKI2021}{Kujawski (2021): interpretation and modification of the SWT function}\label{sheet:KUJAWSKI2021}

\block{Source} D.~Kujawski, \emph{Fatigue Fract.\ Engng Mater.\ Struct.} 44(8) (2021) 2202--2209
\cite{kujawski2021}, Eqs.~(9)--(13b), pp.~2205--2207; Figs.~7--11.

\block{Idea and scope} The SWT product $\sigma_{\max}\varepsilon_a$ is read as the total damaging
energy density $W_D$ in the first quadrant of shifted ``damaging'' axes (Eq.~9). Under a negative
mean stress the stabilised hysteresis loop keeps about one third of the mean, so the SWT maximum
stress is augmented by $\lvert\sigma_m\rvert/3$. The function equals SWT for $\sigma_m\ge0$ and is
more damaging for $\sigma_m<0$, where SWT is non-conservative; compression--compression cycles can
be damaging. Proposed for uniaxial $R$-ratio effects (ductile iron, 7075-T651). The paper is
uniaxial.

\block{Formulation} Kujawski (Eqs.~11a--11b):
\begin{equation}
W_D = \sigma_{\max}\,\varepsilon_a \quad (\sigma_m\ge0),\qquad
W_D = \Bigl(\sigma_{\max}+\frac{\lvert\sigma_m\rvert}{3}\Bigr)\varepsilon_a \quad (\sigma_m<0) .
\label{eq:kuj21-main}
\end{equation}
The stress-based forms (Eqs.~12a--13b) are
$\sigma_{ar}=\sqrt{\sigma_{\max}\sigma_a}$ for $\sigma_m\ge0$,
$\sigma_{ar}=\sqrt{(\sigma_{\max}+\lvert\sigma_m\rvert/3)\,\sigma_a}$ for $\sigma_m<0$, and, with
the strain amplitude, $\sigma_{ar}=\sqrt{E\varepsilon_a(\sigma_{\max}+\lvert\sigma_m\rvert/3)}$.
The repository applies Eq.~\eqref{eq:kuj21-main} on every plane:
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{DP} = \max\Bigl(\sigma_{n,\max}+\frac{\lvert\sigma_{n,m}\rvert}{3}\,[\sigma_{n,m}<0],\ 0\Bigr)\,\varepsilon_{n,a},
\qquad \varepsilon_{n,a}=\tfrac12\lvert\Delta\varepsilon_{zz}\rvert .
\label{eq:kuj21-plane}
\end{equation}
\emph{Critical plane}: the plane of largest normal strain range, the plane rule of SWT
(sheet~\ref{sheet:SWT}); ties go to the larger DP. \texttt{KUJAWSKI2021\_ext} is the largest DP over
all planes. \emph{Shear amplitude}: none is used, so \texttt{np\_shear\_rule} (default
\texttt{rss}) and the global selection do not change the value.

\block{Constants and calibration} Nothing is fitted in the function. The paper's life curve is the
fully reversed ($R=-1$) $\sigma_{ar}$-- or $W_D$--life curve of the material; the repository fits
only the master curve, on the uniaxial $R=-1$ design tests, where $W_D$ equals the SWT value.

\block{Life} The paper reads $\sigma_{ar}$ or $W_D$ on the fully reversed curve against $2N_f$. The
repository uses the master curve on $W_D$, Eq.~\eqref{eq:mastercurve}.

\block{Repository choices}
\begin{itemize}
\item Multiaxial extension (maintainer decision): the source is uniaxial; the function is applied
  on the planes of the repository's SWT critical-plane extension, with the normal stress and strain
  of each plane. Without a compressive normal mean on the plane it is identical to SWT.
\item The bracket $\sigma_{\max}+\lvert\sigma_m\rvert/3=\sigma_a-2\lvert\sigma_m\rvert/3$ is clipped
  at zero, as SWT clips a fully compressive cycle: $\mathrm{DP}=0$ when
  $\lvert\sigma_m\rvert\ge1.5\,\sigma_a$.
\item The applied mean stress is used: the factor 1/3 already stands for its stabilised relaxation,
  and the strains are linear elastic.
\item DP is $W_D$, not $\sigma_{ar}$ (a monotone transform); life from the shared master curve.
\end{itemize}

\block{Worked example} 7075-T651, $E=71\,700\MPa$, $\nu=0.33$; uniaxial cycles:
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}rrccc@{}}
\toprule
$\sigma_a$ (MPa) & $\sigma_m$ (MPa) & $W_D$ (MPa) & SWT (MPa) & $\sigma_{ar}=\sqrt{EW_D}$ (MPa)\\\midrule
200 & 100 & 0.8368 & 0.8368 & 244.9\\
200 & 0 & 0.5579 & 0.5579 & 200.0\\
200 & $-90$ & 0.3905 & 0.3068 & 167.3\\
150 & $-180$ & 0.0628 & 0.0000 & 67.1\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
For $\sigma_m=-90\MPa$, $\sigma_{ar}=\sqrt{(110+30)\cdot200}=167.3\MPa$ (Eq.~12b), against
$\sqrt{110\cdot200}=148.3\MPa$ for SWT. The compression--compression cycle ($\sigma_{\max}=-30\MPa$)
is damaging, as in Fig.~10. In phase, $\sigma_a=150$, $\sigma_m=100$, $\tau_a=90\MPa$: 0.7879 for
both KUJAWSKI2021 and SWT.
